## 🧭 About

I like building things that touch every layer of the stack — from encrypted transports and distributed runtimes underneath, to native desktop and mobile frontends on top. Most of my work sits at the intersection of **systems programming** and **product engineering**: Rust on the backend, Swift and Kotlin at the edges, TypeScript everywhere else.

**What I care about:**

- ⚙️ **Systems** — performance, encryption, and infrastructure that stays out of your way
- 📱 **Native craft** — desktop and mobile apps that feel like they belong on the platform
- 🤖 **Applied AI** — retrieval pipelines and agents that do something genuinely useful
- 🏆 **Shipping fast** — hackathon deadlines are my favorite way to learn
